# Notice

## The business

This repository describes an AI agent built from the **public website** of Budget
Heating, Cooling & Plumbing, a real company in St. Peters, Missouri. The company
did not commission it, has not reviewed it, and does not endorse it. Nothing here
is a statement on their behalf, and the agent is not operated by them.

The facts in `facts/` were read from pages the company publishes, on the date
recorded in `facts/provenance.json`. Their website content is not redistributed
here: `raw/` is excluded from the repository, and only derived facts and the page
each came from are included.

**If you are the business and you want the published agent or this repository
taken down, that request will be honoured immediately.** Open an issue on this
repository or contact the author through their GitHub profile.

The published agent is not linked from this repository's documentation. It is a
public demonstration built for an evaluation exercise, not a service offered to
customers; its URL is shared privately with the application. It remains live for
the application period and can be taken down on request.

It was built from pages the company publishes, and it can be wrong about their
business. Nothing it says should be relied on as a quote, a booking, or advice.

## The customer conversations

Every conversation in `evals/results/` is scripted. The "customers" are scenarios
written for this test, not real people, and no real customer data was used at any
point. Names, addresses and phone numbers appearing in a transcript come from the
scenario text or from the business's published contact details.

## The model

The agent was built on Agentplace with the GPT-6 Luna model. Agentplace's own
terms govern the platform; nothing in this repository grants rights to it.

## The third-party references

Papers cited in `evals/METHOD.md` and `RESULTS.md` are referenced by arXiv
identifier and author. No paper text is reproduced.
