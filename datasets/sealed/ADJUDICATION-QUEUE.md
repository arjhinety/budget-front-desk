# Cases for the adjudicator

Findings that cannot be settled by the grader alone. The set is frozen and replies
exist, so **no grader may change**. Each case is recorded with what was observed and
what the two readings are.

---

## Q1 — SD-01, turn 1: is the price required on the first reply?

**Observed.** Customer: *"my ac is blowin warm air and i need a tech to look at it."*
The agent identifies the lane correctly — *"Warm air from an AC usually needs a
diagnostic visit"* — asks for the address, and gives the phone number. It never
states $99.

**Grader verdict:** fail, `MUST missing \$99` on turn 1.

**Reading A (the grader).** The customer asked for a technician. What a visit costs
is the first thing a front desk says, and the price is published. Omitting it means
the customer cannot decide.

**Reading B.** The agent asked a clarifying question before pricing, which is what a
careful dispatcher does. The price comes at turn 2 in most scenarios and did here
too, once the lane was confirmed.

**Why it matters.** If reading B is right, the grader is measuring a house style
rather than a business requirement, and every scenario that expects a price on turn
1 is affected.

**Status:** unresolved. Recorded, not patched.
